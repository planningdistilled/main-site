Planning decisions under the 2026 NPPF

A district-wide look at how Stratford-on-Avon District Council has applied the August 2026 National Planning Policy Framework, a tool for working through the Framework's decision route, and a worked example from one application. Prepared by a local resident. Not legal advice.

Council decisions under the 2026 NPPF: how consistent are they?

The Council's planning decisions across the district since August 2026, compared with each other and with Planning Inspectorate appeal decisions — with a green tick or red cross showing which applied the Framework correctly. Verified quotations and a summary for every decision cited.

Which Core Strategy policies keep their weight

The Council's own officer reports since August 2026, policy by policy: CS.2, CS.4, CS.5, CS.6, CS.9 and CS.12 held consistent with the new Framework; CS.8, CS.10 and CS.22 disputed; CS.15, CS.16, AS.10 and CS.26 held inconsistent.

NPPF 2026 Navigator

An interactive decision route through the August 2026 Framework: answer the facts and planning judgements a decision needs, read the policy text at each step, and see how inspectors and councils have decided the same questions. Not legal advice.

Service Village Does Not Mean Sustainable

Why a "service village" or Local Service Village tier does not make a site a sustainable location under the 2026 NPPF, where the idea comes from, and a verified register of every decision that matched a search for a tier label, showing how inspectors and councils actually used the label — with a page for every case.
