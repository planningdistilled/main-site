Local planning authorities

Reviews of how individual councils have applied the August 2026 NPPF in their own decisions.

Stratford decisions under the 2026 NPPF: how consistent are they?

How Stratford-on-Avon District Council's planning decisions since the August 2026 NPPF compare with each other and with Planning Inspectorate appeal decisions: DP3(3), heritage harm, Core Strategy CS.8, Green Belt villages and walking routes. Verified quotations and case summaries.

Which Stratford Core Strategy policies keep their weight under the 2026 NPPF

Which Stratford-on-Avon Core Strategy policies keep their weight under the August 2026 NPPF, policy by policy, in the Council's own words: CS.2, CS.4, CS.5, CS.6, CS.9 and CS.12 held consistent; CS.8, CS.10 and CS.22 disputed; CS.15, CS.16, AS.10 and CS.26 held inconsistent. Every quotation checked against the officer reports.

Do other councils make Stratford's four errors? Officer reports from 27 councils under the 2026 NPPF

Do other councils make the four errors found in Stratford-on-Avon's officer reports under the August 2026 NPPF? 243 reports from 27 councils scored with one published rubric: the tier error 7% against 24%, the Green Belt route error 8% against 35%, approval recommended after an unaddressed design conflict 10 times against 15. Warwick repeats the pattern; Bromsgrove and Wychavon do not. Every quotation checked.
