Summary

Class Q prior approval for two dwellings was granted. Like-for-like storm repairs to the barn were not "development", so Q1(g) did not bar the scheme. Siting concerns about suburban fencing were dealt with by boundary and landscaping conditions.

What made the difference

The repairs were minor, did not change the building's form, and did not materially affect its external appearance under s55(2). The Framework plays no part in a Class Q appeal.
