Summary

A barn extension larger than the dwelling it served, for hobby use, was refused as overdominant in the open countryside (DL 7-8, 13). Statutory BNG was given little weight as a benefit (DL 16).

What made the difference

Scale relative to both the barn and the house. A smaller extension would give the same sustainability benefit (DL 11).
