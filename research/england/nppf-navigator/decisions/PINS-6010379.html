Summary

Intensifying a small HMO in Ilford to an 11-person large HMO. Dismissed. The location passed TR3 (walkable shops and services, buses, Goodmayes station). But the undersized communal space (significant weight) and an unproven parking and turning layout (TR6(4), moderate weight) substantially outweighed the moderate benefits under S4. This was so even though the HDT results were very low: 24% (2024) and 30% (2025).

What made the difference

Undersized communal space for 11 occupants, and no swept-path evidence. A very poor HDT raised the housing benefit only to "moderate" for a single HMO conversion (DL ¶28, 33).
