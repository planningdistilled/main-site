Summary

A two-child children's home in a Romford semi was allowed. The Council's Sufficiency Needs Assessment showed ongoing need, and it carried more weight than the commissioner's unsubstantiated view that the borough already had enough homes. Amenity and parking effects matched a family house. The Framework changes were "not material" (DL ¶2).

What made the difference

Documentary need evidence beat an unsupported officer comment (DL ¶¶6–8). The small scale (two children) kept activity comparable to a family home (DL ¶12).
