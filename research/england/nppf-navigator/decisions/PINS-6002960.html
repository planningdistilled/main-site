Summary

A BT Street Hub with digital advert screens in a small local centre with green space. Both the planning appeal and the advert consent appeal were dismissed. The hub would be "monolithic" and incongruous (DL ¶11). CO1's substantial weight to connectivity benefits did not outweigh "very significant and long-lasting harm" to character (DL ¶35). There was no harm to the nearby listed war memorial, and noise and safety concerns could be handled by conditions.

What made the difference

CO1's own siting limb ("sited and designed to minimise the visual impact") cuts against the benefit it gives substantial weight to. Visual prominence in a low-scale green setting outweighed that weight (DL ¶34).
