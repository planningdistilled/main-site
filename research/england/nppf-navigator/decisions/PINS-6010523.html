Summary

Extract flue on a former pub in a commercial setting. Allowed: no harm to character, no residential neighbours. There was "no substantive changes in the new Framework relevant to this appeal", so the parties were not consulted (DL ¶2).

What made the difference

Plans submitted at appeal only "more accurately show the ventilation system as originally proposed" and were accepted (DL ¶4). That cured the Council's sole, information-based reason for refusal.
