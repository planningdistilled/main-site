Summary

An oak-framed garden room on the side of a Grade II cottage pair was refused. Its scale would disrupt the balance of the pair and its glazing would contrast starkly with the host (DL 10-11). HE6 was applied and the benefits did not outweigh the harm (DL 16).

What made the difference

Harm to the pair's symmetry and legibility counted even though no historic fabric would be lost (DL 12).
