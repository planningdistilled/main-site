Summary

A veranda built over the River Lemon was dismissed. Although exempt from the sequential test as minor householder development, it would impede Environment Agency access to flood defences and was unlikely to get a Flood Risk Activity Permit (DL ¶11-18).

What made the difference

The EA's specialist objection, given "significant weight" and not rebutted (DL ¶15).
