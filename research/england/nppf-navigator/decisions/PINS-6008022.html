Summary

A detached replacement for a semi-detached house in Hitchin was allowed. The street had lost its uniformity through alterations, and the plot sat next to a gap by a substation. The inspector asked for a mechanism to secure the claimed BNG self-build exemption, and an executed UU was then supplied. The scheme accorded with the plan and DP3, so S3(1)(c) required approval "without delay" (DL ¶19).

What made the difference

The inspector asked the appellant for the self-build UU during the appeal. Without it, the BNG exemption would have failed for lack of certainty (DL ¶12-13). This case uses the S3(1)(c) plan-compliance route rather than S4.
