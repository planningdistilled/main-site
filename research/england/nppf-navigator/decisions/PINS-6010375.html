Summary

Section 73 amendment adding first-floor rooms to an approved backland bungalow. Dismissed for overshadowing and overbearing effect on 19a Brixey Road and overlooking of the approved Unit 2. The decision rests entirely on Poole Local Plan PP27, and the Framework is not cited.

What made the difference

The added height sat directly south of a neighbour's small garden, and there was no daylight or sunlight evidence (DL ¶¶7–8). The s73 added no homes, so the Council's "unmet housing need … does not attract weight" (DL ¶12).
