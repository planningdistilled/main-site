Summary

A driveway near a cul-de-sac junction was allowed despite breaching three technical requirements of the Local Transport Plan. It would not create conflict under TR4(1)(c)(i) and would deter parking on the junction.

What made the difference

The very low traffic and a single vehicle. The Inspector judged actual safety effects rather than the technical guidance (DL ¶¶8–11).
