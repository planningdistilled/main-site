Summary

A replacement self-build house in Little Aston was acceptable on character and design. The appeal was still dismissed because no legal agreement secured self-build status, so the exemption from statutory biodiversity net gain was not established and a net loss would result (DL ¶13-15).

What made the difference

The missing planning obligation. "Without a completed legal agreement I cannot be certain that the appeal proposal would be exempt from BNG" (DL ¶15). The new Framework was not engaged: "the wording of the details most pertinent to this appeal are unchanged" (DL ¶2).
