Summary

A BT Street Hub with digital adverts opposite St Pancras was dismissed. It caused harm to the settings of listed buildings and the conservation area, and it undermined community safety given Metropolitan Police evidence of hub-related crime. Pedestrian safety was acceptable.

What made the difference

The prominent position in an uncluttered stretch of pavement, eroding views of the Grade I station (DL ¶12-13), plus site-specific crime evidence (DL ¶20-22). The letter is dated 17 Aug 2026 but reasons entirely under the 2024 Framework (¶¶212, 215, "less than substantial").
