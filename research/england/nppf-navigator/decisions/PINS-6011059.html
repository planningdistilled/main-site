Summary

Conversion of a house into a 55-child nursery with no off-street parking. Dismissed under TR6 and the local plan because drop-off parking on a congested road would worsen pavement parking. The inspector preferred national car-mode-share data (60-70%) to a small parent survey (26%) (DL ¶14-15). On transition: the Framework is "of 'critical importance'" but "has not materially changed the national planning policy position" on this issue, so there was no consultation (DL ¶2).

What made the difference

Parents park as close as possible, whatever the spare capacity further away (DL ¶16). The proposed parking-management measures were unenforceable.
