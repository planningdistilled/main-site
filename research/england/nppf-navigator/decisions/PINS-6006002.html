Summary

Two extra dwellings at a flatted site in Eastbourne (one in a previously approved rear outbuilding) were allowed. Character and privacy objections were not sustained, and the requested self-build restriction was refused as unnecessary (DL 8, 13, 21).

What made the difference

The outbuilding was already approved and hidden by the fall of the land. Occupiers of a flatted site would expect mutual overlooking (DL 5, 10).
