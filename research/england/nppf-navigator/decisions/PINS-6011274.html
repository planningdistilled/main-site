Summary

An appeal against a blanket obscure-glazing condition on office windows facing a house. Allowed in effect as a split: the condition was lifted for the as-built windows, which matched an unrestricted 2017 approval, and kept for the new wide windows at the boundary. The DM6 condition tests were applied by name (DL ¶5, 8).

What made the difference

Window-by-window assessment against the 2017 baseline.
