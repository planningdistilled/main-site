Summary

A bungalow extension with an extended gable ridge in a Worthing cul-de-sac, appealed for non-determination. Dismissed. The extra bulk above ground-floor level would overshadow the neighbour's only dining-room window, conflicting with DM5 and Framework P3. Under S4, the personal benefits were substantially outweighed (DL ¶15). No consultation on the new Framework was needed (DL ¶2).

What made the difference

The harm fell on a single side-facing window that was the room's only window. The permitted development fallback was less harmful, so it carried very limited weight (DL ¶10).
