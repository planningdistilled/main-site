Summary

Class MA prior approval for a flat above a Leicester commercial parade was dismissed. No noise impact assessment was provided, despite ground-floor commercial uses and multiple flues and plant (DL ¶12-15). The Framework's P3-type aim on noise exposure was applied only to the prior approval matter.

What made the difference

The missing noise impact assessment. Past residential use of the unit did not help, because the building "may not meet modern standards in respect of noise mitigation" (DL ¶14).
