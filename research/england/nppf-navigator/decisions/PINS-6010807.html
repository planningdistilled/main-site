Summary

An equestrian barn with hardstanding in an exposed, elevated open field. Dismissed as "incongruous and visually intrusive" (DL ¶9). Equestrian use "may, in principle, require a countryside location", but that does not excuse inappropriate scale and siting (DL ¶8). The Framework switch was noted without consultation, and the Framework was cited only generically for design.

What made the difference

Siting in an open field with no built context. The nearby cricket pavilion was separated by a road and hedges, so it could not assimilate the barn.
