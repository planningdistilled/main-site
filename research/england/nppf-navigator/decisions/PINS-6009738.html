Summary

A BT Street Hub and its advert panels outside Walton Library were dismissed (with the linked advertisement appeal 6009551). HC4 public-service benefits were given substantial weight. But the unit would clutter an open forecourt that frames the library, and an existing hub nearby already provides most of the functions. Under S4 the benefits were "substantially outweighed" (DL ¶25).

What made the difference

A duplicate facility close by cut the real benefit, and the site's open gateway character was sensitive. The letter paraphrases DP3 as requiring "substantial weight … to compliance with relevant development plan policies when assessing the design quality" (DL ¶24).
