Summary

A three-child care home in a West Derby estate was dismissed. Police evidence of child exploitation and concentrated local crime made the location unsafe for vulnerable occupants, and staff activity would disturb the neighbour (DL ¶12, ¶18). The letter is dated 17 Aug 2026 but applies 2024 NPPF ¶¶96 and 135 (DL ¶11).

What made the difference

The site-specific police objection, which management conditions could not overcome (DL ¶7, ¶12). Contrast PINS-6009133, where a one-child home without such evidence was allowed.
