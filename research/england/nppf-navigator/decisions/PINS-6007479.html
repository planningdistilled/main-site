Summary

Double-glazed replacements for authentic-looking Yorkshire sliding sashes at a Grade II house were refused. The inspector refers to the new Framework's HE6 but uses the 2024 'great weight' and 'less than substantial' language (DL 23-24).

What made the difference

The loss of the local Yorkshire sash style with thick modern frames. Less harmful energy-efficiency options had not been explored (DL 19, 26).
