Summary

A four-person HMO in Moss Side was dismissed on the Core Strategy's protection of family housing in a regeneration area (Policy H11). The inspector found no amenity harm and no excessive HMO concentration (DL ¶10, ¶15). The case was decided plan-led, and the Framework was said to leave the policy context unchanged (DL ¶2).

What made the difference

The Maine Road regeneration objective of retaining family housing engaged the second limb of H11, which was given significant weight (DL ¶11).
