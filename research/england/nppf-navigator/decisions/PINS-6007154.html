Summary

An enlarged replacement house in a 1990s cul-de-sac was refused. Extending forward and sideways removed a staggered layout that kept the frontages open, and the daylight report was unattributed and lacked a methodology (DL 6, 11-12). The letter is dated 17 Aug 2026 and cites the 2024 Framework.

What made the difference

The inadequate daylight evidence and the loss of a layout feature that gave the close its open character.
