Summary

Conversion into six flats in Bournemouth was dismissed solely because heathland mitigation (SAMM and HIP contributions) was not secured, so an adverse effect on the Dorset Heathlands and New Forest could not be ruled out (DL ¶12).

What made the difference

The missing planning obligation. Regulation 63(5) precluded consent.
