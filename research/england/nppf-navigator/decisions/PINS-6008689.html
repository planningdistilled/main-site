Summary

A personal occupancy condition, and conditions requiring the dwelling to be demolished and the land restored when the appellant leaves, were replaced by a single condition tying occupation to employment in the on-site landscaping business. The replacement condition is enough to protect future occupiers from yard noise (DL ¶12-17).

What made the difference

The planning concern was the relationship between the house and the business, not the person occupying it. The Framework is not cited at all. The conditions tests were applied without any policy reference.
