Summary

Converting a garage in the Meads Conservation Area into a studio dwelling was allowed. It preserved the conservation area and gave acceptable living conditions (DL ¶10, ¶17).

What made the difference

A modest conversion of an existing building, rather than the new-build dwelling dismissed in 2025 (DL ¶18).
