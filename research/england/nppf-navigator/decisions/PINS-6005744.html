Summary

Class MA prior approval for four ground-floor flats was refused because one bedroom would have no window. New openings proposed under a separate planning application could not be assumed. The Framework is not cited.

What made the difference

Class MA does not authorise external alterations. Natural light has to be judged on the building as it stands unless the other works are secured (DL ¶¶6, 9–10).
