Summary

Splitting a Huddersfield terraced house into two flats was dismissed. The basement living room of Apartment 1 would have very poor daylight and outlook. Under S4, the P3 harm (substantial weight, "permanent and enduring") substantially outweighed the moderate benefit of one extra home (DL ¶13-14).

What made the difference

A single window behind a porch facing a retaining wall. Whether the flats met NDSS did not matter, because the daylight and outlook failure alone was enough (DL ¶9).
