Summary

A reduced resubmission of rear extensions to a curtilage-listed farmstead cottage still failed. The extensions eroded the plan form that explains its place in the courtyard group, and the heritage assessment could not say what fabric would be lost (DL 14-17). Benefits were largely private (DL 22).

What made the difference

Plan form as evidence of historic function, and the lack of a fabric assessment under HE5.
