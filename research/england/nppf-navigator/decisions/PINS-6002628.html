Summary

A Class MA prior approval for four flats in a former pub building failed because the upper floors were never in Class E use; they retained the pub's sui generis use. The prior-approval matters (conservation area, light, noise) were not reached (DL 10-11).

What made the difference

The planning unit history: the earlier permission changed only the ground and lower ground floors to Class E, so the proposal was not permitted development at all.
