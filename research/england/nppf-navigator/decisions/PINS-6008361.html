Summary

An existing five-person HMO was allowed to become a seven-person sui generis HMO. There was no clustering, no evidence of disturbance or parking harm, and the accommodation was generous. The Inspector noted the new Framework (DL ¶2) but decided the appeal on local policy DMD5.

What made the difference

The change was one additional room, so occupancy stayed below the eight persons in the previously dismissed appeal (APP/Q5300/W/25/3360248), and objectors had no "technical evidence" on parking (DL ¶¶11, 17). The Council's reliance on dwelling space standards for a single letting room was given "little weight" (DL ¶13).
