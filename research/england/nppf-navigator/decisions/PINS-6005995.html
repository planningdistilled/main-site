Summary

A single-storey rear extension adding two units to a 29-unit house in multiple occupation (HMO) in Ealing was dismissed on a non-determination appeal. The extension would sit close to the only or main windows of two existing units, and no assessment showed their daylight, sunlight and outlook would be acceptable. The inspector gave the two units substantial weight under S4 but found the harm, tied to L2(1)(d)(ii), weightier.

Issues and findings

  • Transition. "other changes are not substantive or determinative to the outcome of this appeal. I have therefore not gone back to the parties to seek any comment" (DL ¶2).
  • Living conditions: harm. "In the absence of any substantive evidence to demonstrate otherwise, the proposed development would have a materially harmful effect on the living conditions of the occupants of bedrooms 5 and 30" (DL ¶9).
  • L2(1)(d)(ii): CONFLICT. "The proposed scheme therefore conflicts with the development plan and policy L2 d ii of the Framework. I attach greater than substantial weight to the identified adverse effect of the proposed development" (DL ¶16).
  • Conservation area: no harm. "the proposed extension would not have a harmful effect on the significance of the ECCA and the setting of the CCA" (DL ¶13).

Planning balance

S4(1) is set out (DL ¶14) and the benefits "are afforded substantial weight in favour of the appeal scheme" (DL ¶15). The letter does not then say the benefits are substantially outweighed. It concludes on the plan: "The proposal conflicts with the development plan and the material considerations do not indicate that the appeal should be decided other than in accordance with it" (DL ¶17).

What made the difference

The lack of a daylight and sunlight assessment for the two affected rooms. A site-specific assessment, or a layout that kept clear of the only window of room 5, would have been needed.

Transferable points

  • L2(1)(d)(ii) (acceptable living standards for residents and neighbours) was used as the Framework test for harm to existing occupiers within the same building (DL ¶16).
  • A daylight report prepared for another site does not show that a scheme is acceptable (DL ¶9).
  • Caution: "greater than substantial weight" is not a Framework weight, and the S4 conclusion is not stated in the policy's terms (DL ¶16–17).

  • A daylight and sunlight report for a scheme at 15 Hanger Lane did not assess this proposal (DL 9)
  • Parties not consulted; changes "not substantive or determinative" (DL 2)
