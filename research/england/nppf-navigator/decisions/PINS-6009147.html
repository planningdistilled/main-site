Summary

A B8 unit in Manor Royal was allowed flexible B2/B8 use. A 16-space shortfall against the indicative parking minimum was justified by trip-generation evidence and good public transport.

What made the difference

Undisputed transport evidence showing peak demand of 20 cars, together with the fact that the local standards are expressly "indicative" and allow site-specific justification.
