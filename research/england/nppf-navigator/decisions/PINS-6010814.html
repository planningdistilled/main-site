Summary

A BT Street Hub with digital screens in the Beckenham Town Centre Conservation Area was allowed. Removing the existing kiosk meant the proposals would enhance the street scene (DL ¶10).

What made the difference

A kiosk-removal condition and the town-centre context. The inspector invoked S4's support in principle for development within settlements (DL ¶10). Contrast PINS-6010846 and PINS-6010865.
