Summary

A retrospective rear veranda of polycarbonate and plastic cladding on a traditional semi in the Mead conservation area was dismissed for harm to the host building. It was not visible from the street, so the conservation area was preserved. The letter does not mention the Framework.

What made the difference

The veranda's scale and materials in relation to the host building, even in private views: it "appears as a disproportionate addition that unacceptably dominates the rear" (DL ¶7). This shows that character harm to a host building can be decisive even where no conservation-area harm is found.
