What decides a sustainable location: the factors in decisions

Every factor inspectors and councils have used to decide whether a site is a sustainable location since the August 2026 NPPF took effect, grouped into 11 classes and counted. The walking route decides most often; distance cuts evenly; cycling and the settlement hierarchy are argued but rarely decide.
