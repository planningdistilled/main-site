NPPF 2026 Map

The whole National Planning Policy Framework (August 2026) as a map: the decision route, each chapter as a graph of its policies and the policies they point to, each policy as a tree of paragraphs and limbs, and the Framework's words beside whatever you select. Type a code such as S5(1)(j) or S5 1 j, or a word such as wheeling, to jump to it and see every place the Framework refers to it.

Not legal advice. An aid to reading the Framework. The text in the right-hand column is the Framework's own, checked word for word against the published text when this page was built; the graphs are drawn from the Framework's own cross-references.
