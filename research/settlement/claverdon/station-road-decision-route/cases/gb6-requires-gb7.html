GB6(1): no GB7 category, so inappropriate

Green Belt decisions under the August 2026 Framework that found the proposal failed GB7 and so treated it as inappropriate development under GB6, to be approved only in very special circumstances. The few approvals were on very special circumstances, never by skipping GB7.

Lot B2a, Upper Bourne End Lane, Hemel Hempstead (outdoor wellbeing use and office unit)

Dismissed

Appeal 6011489 · Dacorum · Appeal · 2 Oct 2026

  • GB7(1)(f)(iii) pass the use of the land for mindfulness, stretching, nature-based activity and therapeutic horticulture accepted as recreational and consistent with limb (iii) (DL 6)
  • GB7(1)(f)(iv) fail no reason shown why the building is necessary to support the outdoor use; one-to-one consultations do not depend on a Green Belt location; the building takes a large part of a very small site, so it is not an "appropriate facility"; openness harmed (DL 6-9)
  • GB6(2) harm inappropriate; openness harm from a large glazed office building on undeveloped land; VSC not shown (DL 8-9, 16-18)

A small plot of Green Belt pasture near Hemel Hempstead was to be used for appointment-only outdoor wellbeing sessions, with a prefabricated office unit for consultations. The inspector accepted the use of the land as outdoor recreation under GB7(1)(f)(iii) but found the building was not an "appropriate facility" under (f)(iv): nothing showed it was needed for the outdoor use. The scheme was inappropriate, with added harm to highway safety and character, and very special circumstances were not shown. Dismissed.

  • GB7(1)(f)(iii) fail the limb is not open-ended: because of the words "such as", uses within it take their flavour from the examples given; it does not cover the provision of a static caravan (DL 9)

A static caravan let as a separate home, with its own access, on garden land in the Green Belt at St Leonards and St Ives. The scheme was inappropriate, and one small home did not amount to very special circumstances. Dismissed.
