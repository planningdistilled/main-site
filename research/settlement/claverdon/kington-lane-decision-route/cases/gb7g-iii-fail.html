Failing the location limb makes it inappropriate

Decisions that found the site failed GB7(1)(g)(iii), the sustainable-location limb. Every one treated the scheme as inappropriate development, including those where the land was accepted as grey belt.

55 Boundary Lane, St Leonards and St Ives (static caravan as rental home)

Dismissed

Appeal 6012115 · Dorset · Appeal · 2 Oct 2026 · 1 home

  • GB7(1)(g)(iii) fail bus stops on the A31 0.6 miles (appellant) or over 1 km (Council) away along a mostly unlit pavement; uncontrolled crossing of heavy traffic to the northbound stop; occupants heavily reliant on the car (DL 10-13)
  • GB7(1)(g)(i) not-engaged grey belt and unmet need limbs not decided; (g) failed on limb (iii) alone because all four criteria must be met (DL 13)
  • GB6(2) harm inappropriate; moderate spatial and moderate visual openness harm from a semi-permanent caravan, independent access, comings and goings and domestic paraphernalia; VSC not shown (DL 16-18, 24-25)

A static caravan let as a separate home, with its own access, on garden land in the Green Belt at St Leonards and St Ives. The inspector held that stationing a caravan as a home is not a change in the use of land of the kind GB7(1)(f)(iii) covers, and that the site fails the sustainable location limb of GB7(1)(g) because the walk to the A31 bus stops is long, mostly unlit and ends at an uncontrolled crossing. The scheme was inappropriate, and one small home did not amount to very special circumstances. Dismissed.

28 affordable homes on a grey belt field next to Hatton Station, a small West Midlands Green Belt settlement, 350 m from a railway station reached along unlit Station Road with no footway. The site failed GB7(1)(g)(iii): 28 homes would generate significant movement in this context, the location did not limit the need to travel, and the route to the station was unsafe for pedestrians. Very significant weight to affordable housing (1.96 years' supply) did not clearly outweigh the Green Belt harm.
