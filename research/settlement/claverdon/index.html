Planning in Claverdon

Claverdon, in Stratford-on-Avon District, is a Category 3 Local Service Village in the Core Strategy (Policy CS.15). Because it is washed over by the Green Belt, it is not a settlement as the National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) defines it (Annex B), so housing proposals there are tested under the Framework's Green Belt policies. The Claverdon Neighbourhood Plan was made in December 2019 and forms part of the development plan. Prepared by a local resident. Not legal advice.
