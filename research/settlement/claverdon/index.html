Land North of Station Road, Claverdon

Evidence on the application for five dwellings, prepared by a local resident. Not legal advice. The application, its documents and the comments on it are on the Council's planning portal.

The policies that weigh against the application

National Planning Policy Framework (August 2026)

  • GB6 Green Belt: inappropriate unless it fits a GB7 category; substantial weight to Green Belt harm
  • GB7(1)(g)(iii), TR3 Sustainable location: no genuine choice of transport modes
  • DP3 Design: context, tree cover, and refusal of conflicts without clear justification
  • HE5, HE6 Heritage: harm to Grade II Claverdon Hall and the Conservation Area
  • N2 Landscape character, established trees and hedgerows
  • F7 Groundwater flood risk not addressed

Stratford-on-Avon Core Strategy (keep their weight)

  • CS.5 Landscape · CS.12 Special Landscape Areas (the site is in the Arden Special Landscape Area) · CS.9 Design · CS.6 Natural Environment · CS.4 Flood Risk
  • CS.8 Historic Environment (its weight is disputed)

Claverdon Neighbourhood Plan (keep their weight)

  • NE1 Valued Landscapes (the site is in Valued Landscape A2) · BE1 Design and the Village Design Statement · NE5 Natural Environment · NE2 Flooding
  • BE2 Heritage (its weight is disputed)

Plus the statutory duty to have special regard to preserving the listed Hall's setting (Listed Buildings Act 1990, section 66(1)). What each requires and where the scheme conflicts: the full table on the Station Road Decision Route.
